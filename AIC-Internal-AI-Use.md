# AIC Foundation - AI Usage Recommendation (Draft)

**Adaptive Intelligence Circle Foundation recognizes AI as an important tool for research, development, education, communication, and experimentation.**

However, AIC does not consider AI a replacement for human judgment, responsibility, governance, or meaning-making.

Contributors are encouraged to use AI in a manner that strengthens human agency rather than weakens it.

## > Recommended Uses

+ Research assistance and knowledge exploration.

+ Drafting, translation, localization, and documentation support.

+ Programming assistance and debugging.

+ Rapid prototyping and technical experimentation.
Educational and collaborative work.

## > Discouraged Uses

+ Delegating final governance decisions to AI systems.

+ Presenting AI-generated content as original human research without disclosure.

+ Allowing AI-generated outputs to replace independent reasoning.

+ Automating ethical, governance, or community decisions without human accountability.

## > Principle of Human Responsibility

> AI may assist.

> AI may recommend.

> AI may participate.

> Responsibility remains human.

## > Principle of Meaning Preservation

+ AIC does not view intelligence solely as optimization.

+ Contributors are encouraged to retain space for reflection, disagreement, interpretation, creativity, and human meaning-making.

## > Principle of Transparency

When AI contributes substantially to published content, documentation, or research, contributors should disclose the role AI played in its creation whenever practical.
