# Sponsors

We sincerely thank all individuals and organizations who have partnered with **AIC - Adaptive Intelligence Circle**.

### AIC's Sponsorship Philosophy

AIC operates on the **Zero-Donation core principle**:
We **do not accept any conditional sponsorships** from Big Tech, the government, or any organization that could affect the project's direction and independence.

All support is only accepted if:

- It is completely **transparent**.

- It does not include any strategic conditions, license changes, or governance interference.

- It is solely for **shared infrastructure** and open-source development.

### How You Can Support AIC

**1.** **In-kind Contribution (Highest Priority)**
We highly value contributions of intellect and time:
- Code writing, pull request review
- Documentation, testing, research
- Design, translation, infrastructure support
- In-depth ideas and feedback

**2. GitHub Sponsors** You can provide small, transparent financial support through GitHub Sponsors.

All grants are fully disclosed on [Open Collective](https://opencollective.com/adaptive-intelligence-circle) and GitHub.

**3. Transparent Grants from Organizations** We are open to grants from reputable non-profit, educational, and research organizations (e.g., NLnet Foundation, Sovereign Tech Fund…) as long as there are no strategic ties.

### Financial Transparency

- All income and expenses are publicly disclosed in real time on Open Collective.

- Regular financial reports will be updated in the repository.

- We are committed to using resources solely to serve our mission: building the Ethical Kernel, the Human Meaning Network, and the Third Path Absolute.
