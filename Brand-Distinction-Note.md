## Brand Distinction Note

Adaptive Intelligence Circle (AIC) is **not** AI Circle (USA).

- **AI Circle (USA)**: A think tank founded in 2023 in Washington D.C., focused on AI governance, policy research, and citizen participation. It operates with external funding and publishes policy reports. 

- **Adaptive Intelligence Circle (AIC)**: A covenant-led initiative founded by Nguyễn Đức Trí, rooted in the Global South, shielded by EU and neutral zones. It is ethical-from-kernel, zero-donation, and zero-data. AIC is not a think tank, not a political entity, and not funded by any government or corporate body.

### Key Differences

- **Purpose**: AI Circle (USA) → policy and governance. AIC → covenant, ethical kernel, community inheritance.  

- **Funding**: AI Circle (USA) → external donors. AIC → zero-donation, zero-data.  

- **Narrative**: AI Circle (USA) → expanding the circle of knowledge. AIC → covenant purity, independence from political narratives.  

### Statement

The similarity in names is coincidental. Adaptive Intelligence Circle (AIC) maintains a distinct identity and mission:  

- Ethical-from-kernel.  
- Zero-donation, zero-data.  
- Independent covenant, open for inheritance.  

AIC is a covenant project, not a think tank. Its legacy is designed to remain free from political or financial influence.
